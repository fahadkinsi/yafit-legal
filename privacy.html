YaFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 Nov 2026

YaFit is a training app for personal trainers and their clients. This policy explains what information YaFit handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@yapeople.app.

1. Information YaFit handles

  • Account details (when you create an account): your email address, name and a password. Passwords are handled by Amazon Cognito; YaFit never sees or stores them in readable form.
  • Training data entered by the trainer: client names, birth year, height, goals, health notes, body measurements, training days, routines, workouts, sets, weights, reps and personal records.
  • Client logins: if a trainer invites a client, the client's email address and name, so the client can sign in and see their own training.
  • App settings such as your trainer name, gym name and weight unit.

YaFit does not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or payment details, and it contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

2. How the information is used

  • To run the app: show your clients, log workouts and track progress.
  • To back up your data and keep it in step across your phones (cloud sync).
  • To let a client sign in and view their own training records.
  • To send account emails: verification codes, password resets and client invitations.

Your information is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

3. Where it is stored

Without an account, everything stays on your phone. With an account, data is stored with Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden) region, encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Each trainer's data is kept separate; one trainer can never see another trainer's data, and a client login can only see that client's own records.

The app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your device's IP address when fonts are fetched.

4. Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers that run YaFit (Amazon Web Services for storage, sign-in and email; Google Fonts for fonts), and only as needed to provide the app, or when required by law.

5. Health information

Trainers may record health notes and body measurements about their clients. Trainers are responsible for having their clients' permission to record this information. It is used only to plan and track training.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account, or the account menu for client logins) or by emailing us. Deleting a trainer account permanently removes that trainer's data and all client logins they created. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 35 days before it is gone completely. See Delete your YaFit account.

7. Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, export or delete it, or ask us about it at support@yapeople.app. A client who wants their records changed or removed can also ask their trainer.

8. Children

YaFit is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
