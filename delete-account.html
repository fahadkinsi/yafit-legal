Delete Your YaFit Account

YaFit, the training app for personal trainers and their clients.

Delete it in the app

Trainers: open YaFit → Profile → Delete Account, then confirm twice.

Clients with a login from their trainer: open YaFit → tap the account icon at the top right → Delete Account, then confirm twice.

Or ask us by email

Send an email from the address you use with YaFit to support@yapeople.app with the subject "Delete my YaFit account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Trainer account: your login, your profile and settings, and all your clients, teams, routines, workouts, measurements and custom exercises, plus every client login you created.
  • Client login: the login only. Your training records belong to your trainer's account; ask your trainer if you want them removed.

What is kept, and for how long

Deletion takes effect immediately. Deleted data can remain in encrypted database backups for up to 35 days, after which it is gone for good. Nothing is kept after that.

See also the YaFit Privacy Policy.
